# My Mini Farm

My Mini Farm is a small farming game made with Python and Tkinter. Plant seeds, grow and harvest crops, sell your produce, and gradually expand your farm. The Windows executable is available to download on itch.io.

## How to play

1. Open the **Shop** and buy seeds that are in stock.
2. Select a seed from the inventory at the bottom of the screen, then click an empty plot to plant it.
3. Select **Water** and click your crop when it needs watering.
4. Wait for it to grow, then select **Harvest** and click the ripe crop.
5. Sell your harvest in the **Marketplace** to earn coins and buy more seeds or upgrades.

Some crops need to be watered more than once. The rare Dragon Fruit takes the longest to grow and needs extra care.

## Features

- A visual farm with multiple plots and different crop growth stages
- Sunny, cloudy, and rainy weather with changing visuals
- Six crops with different shop availability, growth times, and watering needs
- A seed shop that refreshes its stock every 60 seconds
- Two customers per stock cycle who may buy up to three items each from the shop
- A marketplace with sale prices that change over time
- A Crop Library that records discovered plants
- A Mixing Bucket with hybrid crops and recipes that unlock in order
- Tutorial quests, farming challenges, achievements, and a statistics menu
- Animated watering, planting, and harvesting robots
- Farm plot upgrades
- Save files for the packaged Windows game

## Crops

| Crop | Chance to appear in the shop | Possible stock | Growth time |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Carrot | 90% | 1–6 | 40 seconds |
| Tomato | 75% | 1–3 | 60 seconds |
| Strawberry | 55% | 1–3 | 60 seconds |
| Pumpkin | 35% | 1–2 | 90 seconds |
| Golden Flower | 5% | 1 | 120 seconds |
| Dragon Fruit | 1% | 1 | 180 seconds |

Customers buy from the **shop’s stock**, not from the player’s harvest. If they buy a seed you wanted, it will no longer be available until a later restock.

## Download and run

Download the Windows version from the My Mini Farm page on itch.io and run the `.exe` file.
